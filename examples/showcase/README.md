# Showcase

A desktop-shell look built only from Bead's kit widgets and tokens: a translucent top bar (workspaces, a clock, now playing, status icons) and a dashboard panel that slides down from it. The dashboard holds a calendar, a weather tile, three arc gauges, a media card, four quick toggles and two sliders. It is the picture at the top of the [README](../../README.md).

```sh
bead check examples/showcase
bead run examples/showcase
```

- The clock is the real `clock` service. Everything else (workspaces, media, weather, gauges, network, battery) is **demo data** written in `view/`, not live readings.
- Click the clock to open the dashboard; Escape or a click outside closes it.
- The wallpaper is not part of the app: Bead draws the bar and panel, and the compositor shows whatever is behind them. `wallpaper.jpg` is the image the README captures were taken over, and `view/art.png` is the album art.
- Provenance: `wallpaper.jpg`, `view/art.png` and `palette.json` are generated by [`generate-art.py`](generate-art.py) (procedural, seed 7, no photo or third-party image). The palette roles are picked from the wallpaper's own colours.
- Icons: kit components draw glyphs from the font family named by `tokens.fonts.icon`, which is `icons` by default. Without a font of that name installed, glyph names render as their letters. The captures installed a copy of the Material Symbols Rounded subset in `crates/bead-test/fonts/FixtureSymbols.ttf` (Apache-2.0) under the family name `icons`; the repository files are unchanged.
- Fonts: the captures ran in a container that has DejaVu Sans only (its serif and mono faces were deleted from that throwaway container, so a bold request cannot fall back to a serif face). Bead's default `sans-serif` family comes from the host.
