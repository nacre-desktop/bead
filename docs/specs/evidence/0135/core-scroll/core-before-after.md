Settings content is the main remaining frame-cost failure. The 0981 diagnostic attributed its earlier ~19ms first use of buffer slot 2 to full-window repair; the final 3b890 evidence removed that full-window repair, but render p95 remained about 9.6–10.2ms. The later final-bundle measurements above still show content render p95 8.49–10.24ms, so the ≤8ms target remains unmet. Do not interpret the presented p95 alone as a drop count: with `refresh_ns=0`, no physical-vsync/drop-free claim is supported.

The candidate Bead history functional probe at corrected global point (800,300) moved through 20 rows in a 440px viewport (436px scroll limit); reversal was 24.15ms finger and 6.03ms wheel in the earlier 0e56 build. Control history is N/A because the old `Column+Repeat` view was not scrollable. QML has a structurally different right-edge card panel while Bead uses a centered compact dashboard; these are not matched geometry, and QML no-frame observations are not evidence of a Bead-equivalent scroll result. New registration/candidate captures are required for the clipboard route.
