At scale1.5, the logical capture point `(960,800)` maps to screenshot `(480,400)` and lies inside the visible result rows in both shells; the prior y=600 point landed at the panel border. The endpoint screenshots are [QML](launcher-point800/qml-before.png) and [Bead](launcher-point800/bead-before.png). These are inherited parity gaps, not a scrolling-patch regression.

This remains a measured input/state failure, not an accepted scrolling timing result. No private installed-app inventory is included.
