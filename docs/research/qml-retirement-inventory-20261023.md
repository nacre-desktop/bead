# QML retirement inventory — 2026-10-23

Read-only research. Sources: Nacre `origin/main` (tree 421 `.qml` files: about 170 product, rest tests/fixtures/Quickshell mocks), Bead `origin/main` specs B0001-B0041 (after fetch), `docs/research/core-switch-gap-20261022.md`, Nacre `docs/specs/README.md`. B0030 (in-process notifications), B0042 (session confirmations), B0043 (AI sidebar) and B0044 (launcher flip performance) are not on origin/main yet; they are treated as in progress.

Premise (owner): no QML after the switch. Important consequence: the Bead roadmap and B0029 (`nacre-qml-windows.service`, `NACRE_QML_WINDOWS_ONLY=1`) assumed Settings, Colors, Welcome, AI and software stay QML, started on demand. That assumption is now void: each of those needs a Bead (Luau/kit) or native replacement, and B0029's companion unit becomes a temporary bridge only. Biggest single block: the Settings app.

Legend. Bead status: DONE (spec), PARTIAL, MISSING, RETIRE (no replacement needed; owner confirm where marked). Contract: PUB (published and consumed), PUB-GAP (published, a field or method missing), MISS (no contract), N/A (view-only). Size: S under 2 days, M 2-5, L 1-2 weeks of one worker.
